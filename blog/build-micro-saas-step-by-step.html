Build Micro SaaS Step by Step: From Idea to First Paying User

Published June 3, 2026 · 8 min read

This is the roadmap to build micro SaaS without skipping the steps that matter. Each phase has a clear done state before you move on—so you are never “almost ready” for months.

Step 1 — Idea (1 day): One niche, one problem, one sentence solution. Write it down. Ask five potential users if they would pay $10/month. Three yeses means go.

Step 2 — Prototype (3–7 days): Use Cursor to build micro SaaS v1—often a Chrome extension popup plus one action on a target site. No payments yet. Demo to those five people.

Step 3 — Polish (3–5 days): Fix bugs they mention. Improve onboarding text. Add an icon and basic branding so it feels real.

Step 4 — Payments (2–3 days): Stripe test mode, one paid tier, clear upgrade button. Test the full flow yourself twice.

Step 5 — Launch (1 week): Store listing or landing page live. Post where your niche gathers. Email everyone who said yes in step 1.

Step 6 — Iterate (ongoing): Ship small improvements weekly. Reply to support within 24 hours. That is how micro SaaS compounds.
