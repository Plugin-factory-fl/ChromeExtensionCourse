Common questions

I’ve never built a browser tool before. Is this for me?

Yes. Part 1 starts from zero. You’ll follow along with Cursor until something real appears in Chrome—before any advanced topics.

Do I need to know how to code?

No formal training required. If you’re comfortable installing apps and following steps on screen, you can do this. Cursor writes most of the code; you guide and test it.

What do I need on my computer?

Google Chrome and the Cursor app. Later lessons walk you through optional extras (like accepting payments) only when you’re ready.

Can I go at my own pace?

Absolutely. All lessons are on demand. Revisit any video whenever you’re working on your next idea.
